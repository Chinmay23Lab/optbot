# Indicator study

Exits are the same for every rule: trailing stop of N x ATR (5-min), closed by 15:10 at the latest. One trade per index per day, entries 09:30-14:00. Costs: ₹250 per lot round trip. Rupees assume a 1-lot debit spread moving about 0.4 x the index.

- NIFTY: 680 sessions (2023-11-20 to 2026-11-17), test period from 2025-08-13, lot 65, cost hurdle 9.6 pts/trade
